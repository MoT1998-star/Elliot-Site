Design

Every detail is drawn before it is made.

Our process is seamless — from first sketch to final installation. We work alongside architects, designers, and builders to match material, finish, and mechanism to the room it will live in.

Our process

Concept, specification, installation.

We begin with a conversation about the space, the finish, and the intent behind it. From there, we specify hardware from our wall of manufacturers or, where nothing existing is right, work with our house collection to build something bespoke.

01 — Consult

A conversation, not a catalog.

Every project starts with a walk through material, finish, and mechanism — in our showroom, by appointment.

02 — Specify

Sourced from the right maker.

We draw from a curated wall of 100+ manufacturers to match the right hardware to the right room.

03 — Install

Seamless, start to finish.

Our team stays with the project through delivery and installation — the smaller the detail, the more it matters.
