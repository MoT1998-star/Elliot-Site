About

Four generations, one showroom.

Amalfi Hardware has been family-owned since 1952. What began as a single counter of imported fittings is now a showroom of more than 100 brands — the business has grown, the attention to detail has not.

Our story

Started with a single counter.

Giovanni Amalfi opened the first showroom in 1952, importing fittings from workshops he'd trained in as a young man. Three generations later, the business he started is still family-run — still built on the same idea: the smaller the detail, the more it matters.

Heritage

2026

Fourth generation, same showroom floor.

Evelyn Dweck and Elliot Beyda lead the business today, still by appointment only.
